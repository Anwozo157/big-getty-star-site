How it works

Four steps.
One closed finding.

  1. Step 1

    Scope & authorize

    You name the accounts, the time window and the contact. He confirms before touching anything.

  2. Step 2

    Assess

    He reviews configuration, identity, data exposure and logging against CIS Benchmarks.

  3. Step 3

    Report with fixes

    Findings are ranked by severity, and each one lists the exact change to make.

  4. Step 4

    Verify & harden

    He re-checks after you fix it, so a closed finding stays closed.
